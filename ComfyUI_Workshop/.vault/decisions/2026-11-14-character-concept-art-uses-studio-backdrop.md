# Character concept art uses the clean studio backdrop — no background

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Status:** active
**Decision:** Character concept art in this workspace is rendered against the **seamless
neutral silver-grey studio backdrop** used in rounds 01 and 02. No environment, no scenery,
no ground detail, no props. Just the subject and a soft contact shadow.

The house backdrop, verbatim:

> seamless neutral silver-grey studio backdrop, plain and empty, smooth vertical gradient
> from light grey at the top to mid grey at the floor, soft contact shadow pooled under the
> subject, no landscape, no sky, no horizon, no scenery, no buildings, no props

**Reason:** the user stated it directly — *"for character concept art, i dont need any
background, i prefer the background of round 1 and 2"*. It was given after round 03 went
wrong.

**The backdrop also guards the category.** Round 03 changed two things at once — the
subject (industrial vehicles instead of mechs) and the backdrop (a ruined city instead of a
studio). The result read as vehicles. An environment gives the model permission to build a
machine that belongs to that place; the studio backdrop takes that permission away and asks
for **a design**, which then has to carry the war-machine read on its own.

**Consequence for "look completely different" rounds:** change the design language, the
finish and the camera. **Never** the subject category, and **never** the backdrop.

**Applies when:** generating character or mech concept art for this workspace. A task that
genuinely needs an environment is a different brief and should be confirmed first.

round-03 failure.
