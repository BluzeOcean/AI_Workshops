# A vague keep-list is worse than none — describe the reference's CAMERA, POSE and MATERIAL, measured from the picture

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** 13 (rejected) and 14 (the correction)
**Status:** applies to every edit-path concept

---

## What happened

Round 13 took the user's final S01 design and made class variations of it — three snipers and
three stealth backstabbers — through the reference-IMAGE edit path. All six were rejected:

> *"I don't like the R13 generations. They look like the Mac in the 80s, more like a plate armor
> than a mech machine."*

The renders came back as squat, upright, orange-and-cream figures with rounded overlapping
discs for shoulders and a smooth, moulded, toy-like finish.

## Why — the cause was the keep-list, not the model

Round 13's keep-list was **wrong about the reference**, in four ways. Everything it said was
read as an instruction to *change* the machine to match the words:

| Round 13 said | S01 actually is | What the model drew |
|---|---|---|
| "relaxed upright standing pose" | hunched prowling stance, torso pitched forward | upright, mannequin-stiff |
| "overlapping **curved** armour plates" | flat **angular faceted** slabs with rivet rows | rounded overlapping discs — a knight's pauldron |
| "broad heavy frame" | compact and dense, ~5.5 heads tall | the model's own stocky mannequin |
| *(camera never mentioned)* | high three-quarter view looking **down** at the machine | the default straight-on eye-level front view |

The "relaxed upright standing pose" was the worst of them: it came from the house rule in
`2026-11-14-concept-art-wants-a-relaxed-standing-pose.md`, which is a **text-path** rule. On the
edit path the reference's own pose **is** the pose — asking for an upright stance asked for a
change of pose, and the model complied.

The camera is the silent one. Round 13 never mentioned it, so the model used its default
straight-on view — and a hunched machine photographed from eye level reads as a toy figurine on
a table.

**A vague keep-list is worse than no keep-list.** Silence means "the model fills the gap with
the house average"; a vague keep-list means "the model fills the gap with the house average
*according to these wrong words*". Round 13's failure was not that the keep-list was too
short. It was that it was **specific and false**.

## The fix

Write the keep-list from **the picture**, measured, and cover these six things every time:

1. **The camera** — where the camera is and what it sees. *"the same high three-quarter front
   view from above with the camera looking down on the machine at a steep angle so the tops of
   its shoulders and its back are visible and the whole machine sits small in the frame with air
   around it"*.
2. **The pose** — the actual stance, joint by joint. *"hunched prowling stance with the torso
   pitched forward, the head thrust forward and down between the raised shoulders, the arms
   hanging down and forward with heavy clenched grippers, and the short thick legs crouched and
   splayed wide with the broad feet planted far apart"*.
3. **The proportions** — measured, not stock phrases. *"compact dense proportions, about five
   and a half heads tall, short thick legs and a broad deep torso"*.
4. **The plate shapes** — flat or curved, faceted or moulded, and where they sit. *"the
   overlapping flat angular shoulder slabs stepping down and out over the upper arms with rivet
   rows along their edges"*.
5. **The material vocabulary** — the words that kill the plastic read. *"hard-edged bevelled
   steel armour, angular and faceted with sharp corners and no rounded moulded forms … the
   exposed black mechanical structure between the plates — hydraulic pistons, actuators, hose
   bundles and cable looms"*.
6. **The head** — size, shape and where it sits. *"the small dark cowled head module thrust
   forward and low with a narrow dark visor and two amber optic eyes"*.

Round 14's keep-list, corrected on all six, produced **six good renders on the first try** —
the only round in this series that has ever done that.

## The negative has to carry the drift

Round 14's negative gained the failure modes that round 13 walked into:

```
upright posture, standing straight, vertical stance, facing the camera squarely,
plate armour, knight armour, medieval armour, samurai armour, paladin,
action figure, toy, figurine, mannequin, model kit, plastic model,
smooth plastic, glossy plastic, injection moulded plastic, beige plastic,
consumer electronics, retro appliance, retro computer, vintage computer case,
soft rounded moulded shapes, rounded discs, rounded pauldron discs,
balloon, bulbous, flat blank panels, large untextured surfaces,
human face, anime face, cartoon face, cute face
```

The floor measured **evenly lit on every round-14 probe** (bottom-left excess between −3.6 and
+3.5, threshold 12) — the house backdrop survives the edit path unchanged.

## Applies when

- Any edit-path concept whose reference is a **picture of a specific machine**.
- Whenever a keep-list is written from a **description of the reference** rather than from the
  reference itself. Open the picture, measure it, write it down.
- And see `2026-11-14-class-hardware-must-use-the-machines-own-vocabulary.md` — the second half
  of the round-14 fix.
