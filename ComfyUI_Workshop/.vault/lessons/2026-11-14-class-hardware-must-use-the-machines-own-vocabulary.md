# Class hardware must be built from the MACHINE'S own construction vocabulary

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** 13 → 14
**Status:** applies to every edit-path concept that adds hardware

---

## What happened

Round 13 made the S01 design read as a sniper by adding sensor hardware. The wording was
ordinary and reasonable:

- *"a bulky rectangular rangefinder pod with a big glass lens at its front is bolted flat onto
  one shoulder"*
- *"a long horizontal row of small glass sensor lenses is set into a recessed strip across its
  upper chest plate"*

The renders came back with a **grey box with a camera lens** on the shoulder and a **chrome
speaker grille** across the chest. Consumer appliance, not war machine.

## Why

A new part that is described in **borrowed** vocabulary inherits that vocabulary's whole
associations. "Rectangular pod, big glass lens" is the vocabulary of a camera, a scope or an
optical sensor — smooth, consumer, clean. The reference's own vocabulary is **hard-edged
bevelled steel, angular and faceted with sharp corners, dusty, chipped at the edges, with
exposed black mechanical structure**. Two worlds apart.

The edit model builds the new part the way it builds the kept parts: in the reference's
material. So the fix is not "make the pod more military" — it is **build the pod out of the
machine's own materials**.

## The fix

Describe class hardware with the machine's construction words, not the class's product words:

| Round 13 (borrowed vocabulary) | Round 14 (the machine's own vocabulary) |
|---|---|
| "bulky rectangular rangefinder pod with a big glass lens" | "a slim folded sensor mast — a narrow vertical column of dark hardware with a small glass lens at its top" |
| "long horizontal row of small glass sensor lenses set into a recessed strip" | "a long cylindrical sighting pod with a glass lens at its front end … mounted on a short black bracket, angled forward and down" |
| — | "a long low ranging scope cowled directly above the visor — a narrow horizontal tube of dark hardware with a glass lens at its front end, on a short arm" |

The words that carry it: **dark hardware**, **narrow column of dark hardware**, **short black
bracket**, **cowl**, **faceted wedge of dark steel**, **short**, **narrow**, **low**. Words
that break it: **bulky**, **rectangular pod**, **big lens**, **unit**, **box**, **module**,
**housing** (unless it is a machined recess).

The same rule applies to the stealth branch's new parts — a "flat blade-shaped shroud of dark
steel" reads as armour, a "blade-shaped fin" reads as a toy.

## Where the class hardware goes

**Not on the head**, unless the reference's head is the right place for it. Round 13 probe 1
asked for a telescopic optic in the visor **and** to keep the two amber optic lenses, and the
model resolved the conflict by drawing one wide cartoon visor band. Class hardware goes on the
**chest, shoulder, forearm or back**; the head keeps its own optics.
